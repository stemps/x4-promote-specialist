# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Right-click a pilot, captain, station manager, ship trader, service or marine
  crew member in the map's object info, or a ship or station itself, and choose
  "Promote specialist" to fill the post with someone from any of your ships.
- Choose from service crew, marines and, for ship pilots, the pilots of your
  other ships, or, for station managers, the managers of your other stations; filter by star rating, preview each candidate with all their
  skills, and step through them (best to worst) before promoting.
- The person replaced either swaps to the specialist's ship and role, or stays
  aboard as service crew when there is room.
- Taking another station's manager swaps the two managers (via a short stop
  as crew on a nearby ship).
- Taking another ship's pilot exchanges the two pilots, or keeps the replaced
  one aboard and promotes the best crew on the other ship to pilot.
- Empty pilot and manager posts offer "Promote specialist" too.
